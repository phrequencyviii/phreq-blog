I have set up a new blog and personal website...and for once, it's completely up to my needs and wants. I've run the gamut with CMS and blog systems in my time, and for those interested, I am now using Astro. <https://astro.build>

I love the concept of static websites, mostly because they are fast and easily maintained with just a text editor. I am so guilty of building stuff, tearing it down, and then building it again, but I am very happy with how this is coming along, so we will see how durable this blog is. 😄
